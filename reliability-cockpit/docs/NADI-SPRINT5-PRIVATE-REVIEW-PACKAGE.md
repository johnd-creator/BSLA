actual candidate React components at a fully intercepted `.invalid` origin with
synthetic fixtures; it is not localhost or factual plant evidence. Mutation/review/
draft persistence is qualified separately by actual HTTP/disposable PostgreSQL
suites, not by a mocked browser save response.

scratch artifacts are not durable operational backups; the owner can copy them
into their approved private evidence store. CI uploads non-image synthetic
